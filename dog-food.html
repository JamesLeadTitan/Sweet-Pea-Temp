Made For Us, And Nobody Else

Our food is made exclusively for SweetPea's in Snowdonia, North Wales, by Pero, using British made ingredients. You will not find these recipes anywhere else in the world.

Every recipe is natural, balanced and complete, so it is everything your dog needs in the bowl. As vet nurses we wanted food we could recommend without any small print, and this is it.

  • Natural, balanced and complete
  • Grain free, and free from wheat, gluten and dairy
  • British made ingredients, responsibly sourced
  • Supports healthy digestion and shiny coats
  • For all life stages and all breeds
  • Cruelty free, from a PETA registered maker
