About Us

Two Vet Nurses Who Got Fed Up Reading Labels

SweetPea's is run by Claire and Lucy, both qualified veterinary nurses. We kept getting asked the same question in practice: what should I actually be feeding my dog? So we built a deli that answers it properly, and then we made our own food.

Every chew, treat and recipe on our shelves is one we would give our own dogs. If we would not feed it, we do not sell it.
  • Qualified veterinary nursesFeeding advice from people who trained for it, not a sales pitch.
  • Our own recipeMade exclusively for us in Snowdonia, and sold nowhere else.
  • We come to youRegular pitches across Tewkesbury, Cheltenham and Worcester every week.

Fitted By Us

Perfect Fit Harnesses

We are a proud PerfectFit stockist. The Y shaped front spreads any pulling through the chest instead of the soft tissue of the neck and throat, and nothing cuts across the front legs, so your dog can move properly on a walk.

Our team are fully trained to measure and fit, and no appointment is needed. Just bring the dog and allow about half an hour.
