What We Can Bring

Two Ways To Have SweetPea's At Your Event

Dog shows, charity days, fetes, farm shop open days and Christmas markets. We can bring the Doggy Deli, a scentwork have-a-go with Lucy, or both together. Tell us about it using the form and we will come back to you.

The Doggy Deli Pop-Up

Our stall, stocked with our own recipe dog food, natural chews, treats and the pick and mix. Two vet nurses on hand for the questions people always have about what to feed their dog.

  • Food, chews, treats and pick and mix
  • Feeding advice from qualified vet nurses
  • Perfect Fit harnesses, ask us when you book

Scentwork Have-A-Go With Lucy

Lucy is a Bronze and Silver qualified UK Sniffer Dog instructor. She can run a have-a-go arena for visitors' dogs, or come along as the invited scentwork trainer for your event.

  • Short taster searches, any breed, any age
  • Reactive and nervous dogs handled with care
  • Great crowd puller at dog shows
