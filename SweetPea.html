About Us

Two Vet Nurses Who Got Fed Up Reading Labels

SweetPea's is run by Claire and Lucy, both qualified veterinary nurses, with Enrichment Coach Cat alongside them. We started because we kept being asked the same question in practice: what should I actually be feeding my dog. So we built a deli that answers it properly.

Every chew, treat and recipe on our shelves is one we would give our own dogs. If we would not feed it, we do not sell it.

Perfect Fit Harnesses

We are a proud stockist of PerfectFit. The Y shaped front spreads the pulling force across the chest instead of the soft tissue of the neck and throat, and nothing cuts across the front legs, so your dog can actually move properly on a walk.

  • Built in three piecesModular, so we mix and match sizes to fit any shape, age or breed.
  • Measured and fitted by usOur team are fully trained, so you leave with it on and adjusted.
  • Calm, unhurried fittingsWe read your dog's body language and go at their pace, which matters for puppies and nervous dogs.
No appointment needed for a measure and fitting. Just bring the dog and come and find us.

Puppy Club And Enrichment

Puppy Club is our friendly group session for new arrivals, covering socialisation, careful handling and the first bits of enrichment. It is a calm, small group, run by people who look at puppies for a living.

Enrichment Coach Cat also runs sessions at Bennetts Willow Barn, working on the sniffing, licking and chewing that tires a dog out far better than another lap of the park.
